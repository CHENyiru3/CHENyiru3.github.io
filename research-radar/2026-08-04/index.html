SpatialFormer: universal spatial representation learning from subcellular molecular to multicellular landscapes

Nature Computational Science Published 2026-07-29 Research Article DOI: 10.1038/s43588-026-01016-7

Authors: Winther et al.

spatial transcriptomics representation learning cell-cell relationships cell communication

Summary: SpatialFormer is a transformer-based model for spatial transcriptomics that learns cell–cell relationships to predict cell co-localization, annotate cell types and niches, and identify cell communication gene pairs in pulmonary fibrosis.

single-cell multimodal integration continual learning batch correction
