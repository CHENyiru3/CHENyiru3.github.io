Calibrated cell-state models and testable tissue signals

Calibration, statistical assumptions and spatial context shape what cell-state analyses can support. These studies pair model evaluation with regulatory and immune perturbations, while distinguishing inferred communication from experimental causality. Coverage is partial: Nature Computational Science failed strict XML safety validation; the eight PubMed journal feeds are bounded indexed subsets and may lag publication. Preprint and abstract-only limits appear with each affected article.

  • Perturbation-metric calibration, PCA model checks and marker-specificity diagnostics make measurement assumptions explicit.
  • SFUMATO, stFormer and multiomic integration represent uncertainty and context without turning model outputs into causal evidence.
  • Matrix-directed immune niches and cell-specific regulatory perturbations connect tissue states to experimentally tested mechanisms.
  • LACE separates problem specification from complementary heuristic search in a general AI-assisted optimization setting.
