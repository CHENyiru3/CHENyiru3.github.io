Spatial perturbation technologies are maturing into integrated in vivo discovery platforms

Recent additions to Zotero show a concentrated pull toward methods that combine spatial transcriptomics with CRISPR screening in intact tissues — a signal that in situ perturbation mapping is becoming a mature discovery paradigm.

  • Multiple Zotero additions combine spatial transcriptomics with in vivo CRISPR screening (CLIM-TIME, Perturb-FISH, Perturb-Multimodal, PerturbView).
  • RAEFISH and Perturb-DBiT push spatial readout into whole-genome and in vivo territory.
  • CRISPRmap adds multimodal phenotype mapping on top of perturbation.
