Hot Topic

Representation learning is becoming the dominant paradigm in single-cell and spatial omics

The strongest computational signal this cycle is the shift from clustering one dataset to building transferable, interpretable representations that span samples, modalities, and patient-level phenotypes.

  • INSPIRE learns shared and context-specific spatial gene programs via deep learning + NMF across diverse datasets.
  • PaSCient bridges cell-level foundation models to patient-level disease representations from scRNA-seq.
  • Dango uses self-attention hypergraph neural networks to predict higher-order genetic interactions at scale.
  • Zotero collection patterns reinforce interest in perturbation-aware spatial models and foundation-model priors for tissue biology.
