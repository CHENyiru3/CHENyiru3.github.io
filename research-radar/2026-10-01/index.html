From cell-state maps to testable mechanisms

Today's strongest studies connect spatial and multimodal cell representations to measurement controls and targeted perturbations, alongside experimentally evaluated AI-guided design. Coverage is partial: Nature Cell Biology's feed failed XML safety validation; eight PubMed-indexed journal feeds can lag and are bounded subsets, even with all 1,600 metadata records verified.
