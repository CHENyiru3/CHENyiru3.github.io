    summary: >-
      Systematically evaluated single-cell transcriptomic representations in zero-shot settings
      across 20 methods, 6 downstream tasks, and 1,607 datasets comprising nearly 21.8 million
      cells. The large-scale analysis reveals a decoupling between utility and robustness: methods
      ranking highly on standard benchmarks often show marked instability under shifts in dataset
      structure, and no single model performs uniformly well across tasks. Critically, classical
      statistical representations based on highly variable genes remain competitive under zero-shot
      conditions, defining the practical boundaries of zero-shot use in single-cell foundation models.
    why_it_matters: >-
      This is the most comprehensive zero-shot benchmark of single-cell foundation models to date,
      providing a much-needed reality check on the current generation of scFMs. The finding that
      simple HVG-based representations remain competitive has immediate practical implications for
      researchers choosing tools for single-cell analysis, and the decision framework for
      representation selection will guide both users and model developers.

      Directly informs tool selection for spatial omics and single-cell computational immunology
      analyses — the benchmark includes tasks relevant to cell type annotation, batch integration,
      and embedding evaluation that are core to TME and digital twin workflows.

    summary: >-
      Introduces VCBench, a benchmark synthesizing four independent virtual-cell frameworks into
      seven capability dimensions: perturbation response prediction, cross-species universality,
      gene regulatory network inference, modality integration, temporal dynamics, multi-scale
      integration, and in silico experimentation. Simple linear baselines match or exceed every
      foundation model on four of five testable dimensions, with only TranscriptFormer showing
      meaningful improvement on cross-modal RNA-to-protein prediction. The benchmark also exposes
      a critical tradeoff: the architectural choice enabling TranscriptFormer's cross-modal
      advantage simultaneously causes spectral collapse that destroys temporal-ordering performance.
    why_it_matters: >-
      VCBench provides the most rigorous multi-dimensional evaluation framework for single-cell
      foundation models, moving beyond single-task benchmarks that obscure model limitations. The
      finding that foundation models largely fail to outperform simple baselines challenges the
      narrative of rapid progress and establishes a higher bar for claiming meaningful advances.
    why_for_me: >-
      The virtual cell evaluation dimensions — especially perturbation response, GRN inference,
      and temporal dynamics — are directly relevant to building computational models of cellular
      systems for digital twin AI applications in immunology and spatial omics.
